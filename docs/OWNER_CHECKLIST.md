# Founder sign-off record

Record a date, reviewer and evidence link against each relevant item before granting public launch approval. Keep credentials in the provider dashboards.

- [ ] Choose the legal operator, contact email, target jurisdictions, age policy, data retention and deletion handling. Review privacy, terms, acceptable use and refund drafts with appropriate advisers.
- [ ] Document brand and domain clearance and record renewal terms. Verify the domain, DNS, certificate and HTTPS redirects.
- [ ] Read TEST_REPORT.md, SECURITY.md and BUILD_STATUS.md at the exact release commit. Resolve critical/high defects. Verify backup and restore.
- [ ] Confirm the private repository, branch protection, dependency lockfile, secrets separation, database migrations, RLS and administrative role bootstrap.
- [ ] Confirm email verification, password recovery, OAuth if enabled and anti-abuse challenges on the real staging providers.
- [ ] Test two unrelated users, an organizer, a moderator and a signed-out visitor. Check anonymous claim links and all private resources.
- [ ] Exercise all tools individually and inside an event where supported. Check public link, QR, embed, owner-only actions and signed-out participation.
- [ ] Review actual Playwright screenshots, keyboard navigation, contrast, reduced motion and the measured public-page performance report.
- [ ] Check admin audit history, submission approval, removal, reports and suspensions. Confirm a moderator is available during the pilot.
- [ ] Confirm legal copy matches actual analytics, advertising and retention behavior. Configure a deletion contact and data export procedure.
- [ ] Obtain merchant approval for the intended market and currencies. Test verified webhooks, duplicate handling, subscriptions, cancellation, slot exceptions, expiry and refunds.
- [ ] Approve starter guides individually and check canonical URLs, social previews, robots, sitemap and unpublished routes.
- [ ] Keep ads disabled until site approval, CMP integration, regional refusal/withdrawal behavior, reviewed content and placement policy are verified.
- [ ] Review the actual provider quotes and set budget alerts or caps. Record response owners for alerts.
- [ ] Authorize the intended audience, domains and deployment date explicitly. Record release SHA, known limitations and rollback plan.
