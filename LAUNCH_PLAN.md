# Launch and growth plan

## Before announcing

1. Confirm the provisional brand across the operator's home market and the US, UK, Canada and Australia. Check relevant trademark registries, similar names, product classes, domains and social handles. Obtain qualified review when clearance is uncertain. Record the checks and renewal costs before purchasing a domain. Update branding centrally in `lib/brand.ts` and review all marks and social assets.
2. Walk through every release gate in docs/OWNER_CHECKLIST.md. Publish the actual operator contact and reviewed policies. Decide who responds to moderation reports when the founder is offline.
3. Test the event experience using one consenting organizer and a disposable event with clearly labeled demonstration data. Start on a phone from a shared link. Record whether the participant can find the time, complete the intended action and start a project of their own. Address friction before outreach.
4. Validate the intent map in docs/KEYWORD_RESEARCH.md. Write instructions one visitor could follow without assistance. Approve guides individually after testing their tool flows. Prioritize the two or three guides that answer pilot organizers’ recurring questions.
5. Configure Search Console, review sitemap entries, inspect a public canonical URL and check the noindex response of a shared project. Keep incomplete pages, private data and internal search results out of public search.

## Reach organizers

| Channel | Audience and activity | Evidence to collect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discord and Telegram | Ask moderators in established relationships whether they want a short event-planning demo. Use dedicated promotion channels when allowed. Let the organizer decide whether and where to share. | Opt-ins, questions, completed events, repeat use |
| Reddit | Read each community’s rules. Answer a specific organizer question, disclose founder involvement and include a link only when useful and permitted. | Helpful discussion, requested improvements, relevant referrals |
| Developer partnerships | Offer indie developers a reusable event page for a real community playtest. Ask permission before naming the developer or publishing screenshots. | Completed playtests, organizer feedback and voluntary mentions |
| Product Hunt | Prepare a concise product description, actual screenshots, a short accessible demo and an explanation of the first useful action. Schedule only after staffing support and moderation. | Visitors who complete a tool and later return |
| Editorial and organic links | Share a practical guide with organizers who asked for that resource. Offer an embeddable widget when it solves a partner’s task. Let the partner choose whether to link. | Qualitative feedback and referral visits |

Select a single repeatable demo, such as arranging a match, posting its start time and sharing the read-only result. Use fictional teams labeled as demo data. Avoid exposing participant names, moderation records or private management links.

## Weekly founder review

Use production-only aggregates, a fixed weekly window and a written metric definition. Record missing instrumentation as unavailable. Compare tool starts → successful completions → public shares → shared visits → new creators. Count unique people only where a lawful, documented identity signal exists. Multiple hits from the same browser and consent refusal affect attribution. Store feedback voluntarily with minimal personal information.

Track return visits, moderation workload, support tickets and platform costs alongside signups. For monetization, reconcile verified orders, refunds and active subscriptions with the provider. Estimate sponsored performance from clearly labeled views and clicks while excluding known test traffic. Keep organic discovery scores independent. Record each experiment's audience, change, expected behavior, observation and decision. Do not offer guaranteed reach.

Only enable paid promotion after official merchant eligibility, settlement currency, test-mode webhooks, refund handling, availability conflicts, cancellation and expiry have been demonstrated. Launch display ads only after page-level eligibility, CMP behavior and policy approval are checked. Leave spending and live payment decisions with the founder.
